A monthly workflow where an LLM classifies transactions from credit card statement PDFs (three issuers) as confirmed, uncertain or excluded, and outputs a formatted Excel reconciliation for a shared expense fund. A merchant memory workbook keeps classifications consistent across months, and unresolved conflicts are flagged for review.
